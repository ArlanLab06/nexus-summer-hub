# NEXUS Summer Hub
NEXUS Summer Hub is an internal planning website for the NEXUS SDU pre-launch team.
NEXUS SDU is a student-led initiative focused on helping students navigate exchange programs, scholarships, Work & Travel safety, and CV/SOP basics through official sources and practical guidance.

## Purpose
This hub was created to make the summer launch plan easier for the team to understand and follow. Instead of reading a long document, team members can open the website and quickly find the section they need.

## Main sections
- Summer roadmap
- Team roles
- Content plan
- KPI system
- Safety rules
- Fall semester preparation

## Focus areas
- Exchange & Scholarships
- Work & Travel Safety
- CV/SOP Basics

## Status
Pre-launch stage.  
Originally proposed to SDU in December 2025 and developed as a structured initiative starting in March 2026.

## Disclaimer
NEXUS SDU is not an agency and does not provide visa, legal, or paid consulting services. The initiative focuses on official sources, student-friendly guidance, and safety awareness.
